    Questions

    Good to know

    Something else? Ask on GitHub

    Is PricePin really free?

    Yes. It's open source under the MIT license, with no ads, no trial and no paid tier.

    Do I need an exchange account or API key?

    No. PricePin reads public market data only. It never asks for keys, passwords or wallets, and it can't trade.

    Will it work in my country?

    Most likely. If an exchange is blocked where you are, PricePin switches to another one by itself. You can also pick the source in Settings.

    What does it send over the internet?

    It downloads prices, charts and news from public sources. Once a day it sends one anonymous count with the app and Windows version, which you can turn off. Your coins, alerts and holdings never leave your PC.

    Is there a Mac or Linux version?

    Not yet. PricePin is built for Windows 10 and 11, on both x64 and ARM64.

    How do updates work?

    The installed version updates itself in the background when a new release is out. The portable version is updated by downloading it again.
